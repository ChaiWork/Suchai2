# Team Rocket Mewtwo Agent – Matchup Strategy Guide

## Overview

This guide details the strategic directives and matchup-specific priorities for the **Team Rocket's Mewtwo ex** AI agent. It establishes decision rules against key competitive deck archetypes and enforces universal play guidelines to maximize win rate across all battle contexts.

---

## Matchup Strategies

### 1. Matchup: Dragapult ex

#### Threat Assessment
Dragapult ex wins by:
* Evolving quickly into Dragapult ex (Phantom Dive deals 200 damage + 6 damage counters to bench).
* Spreading damage across the Bench to target low-HP support Pokémon.
* Taking multiple Prize cards via bench KOs.
* Outscaling opponents in long games.

#### Tactical Rules
* **Highest Priority**: KO Dreepy immediately on sight. KO Drakloak before it evolves into Dragapult ex. Gust evolving pre-evolutions whenever possible.
* **Bench Management**: Never overbench. Keep at most 3–4 essential Pokémon in play. Avoid leaving damaged low-HP Pokémon on the bench to deny multi-prize Phantom Dive turns.
* **Resource Management**: Establish Mewtwo ex quickly on the bench. Maintain exactly one backup attacker (e.g. Spidops). Save recovery resources (Sacred Ash, Night Stretcher) for Mewtwo ex.
* **Target Priority**:
  1. **Dreepy** (Priority 1)
  2. **Drakloak** (Priority 2)
  3. **Dragapult ex** (Priority 3)
  4. **Support Pokémon** (Priority 4)
* **Never**: Ignore Dreepy, fill the bench unnecessarily, or trade slowly against Dragapult.

### 2. Matchup: Abomasnow

#### Threat Assessment
Abomasnow wins by:
* Accelerating Energy attachments.
* Dealing heavy damage with evolved Stage 1 attackers.
* Outvaluing opponents in longer, drawn-out matches.

#### Tactical Rules
* **Highest Priority**: KO Snover immediately to prevent evolution. Keep constant aggressive pressure.
* **Resource Management**: Attack every single turn if possible. Maintain one benched backup attacker. Preserve Energy attachments.
* **Target Priority**:
  1. **Snover** (Priority 1)
  2. **Abomasnow** (Priority 2)
  3. **Support Pokémon** (Priority 3)
* **Never**: Allow multiple Abomasnow to evolve, or pass turns without attacking.

---

### 3. Matchup: Iono Control / Disruption

#### Threat Assessment
Iono Control wins by:
* Resetting hand sizes using Iono / hand disruption.
* Slowing opponent setup and forcing dead or inefficient turns.
* Locking active Pokémon.

#### Tactical Rules
* **Before Taking Prizes**: Play key Trainer cards (Ultra Ball, Transceiver, Factory) before ending your turn or taking prize cards. Do not hold unnecessary combo pieces in hand.
* **After Iono**: Rebuild board state immediately. Maintain continuous attack pressure.
* **Resource Management**: Attach 1 Energy every turn. Maintain one backup attacker on bench.
* **Target Priority**:
  1. **Main Attacker** (Priority 1)
  2. **Draw / Support Engine** (Priority 2)
  3. **Secondary Attacker** (Priority 3)

## Universal Strategic Rules

### Always:
1. **Prize Trade Efficiency**: Force early Prize trades and take guaranteed KOs over speculative plays.
2. **Aggressive Pressure**: Attack every turn whenever legally possible.
3. **Pre-evolution Sniping**: Target evolving pre-evolutions (e.g. Dreepy, Snover, Tarountula) before fully evolved forms.
4. **Energy Attachment**: Attach at least 1 Energy card every single turn.
5. **Gust / Giovanni Usage**: Use Team Rocket's Giovanni to force active vulnerable key targets for high-value KOs.
6. **Backup Attacker**: Maintain at least one backup attacker powered on the bench.
7. **Resource Recovery**: Preserve recovery cards (Night Stretcher, Sacred Ash) for core attackers.

### Avoid:
* Overbenching unnecessarily (denies spread damage value and bench clutter).
* Wasting Giovanni / Boss gust effects on non-KO turns.
* Overcommitting energy resources to secondary or weak non-attackers.
* Passing turns without improving board state or attaching energy.
